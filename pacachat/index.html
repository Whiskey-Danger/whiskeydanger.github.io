Set it up in three steps

You need a Mac, Windows or Linux computer that stays on, and your phone on the same Wi-Fi. Setup takes about ten minutes, most of it spent downloading a model.

  1. 1

    Run Ollama on your computer

    Install it from ollama.com, then download a model to chat with.

    Terminal
    $ ollama pull llama3.2
  2. 2

    Let your phone reach it

    Out of the box, Ollama only answers the computer it runs on. Start it like this so it listens on your Wi-Fi.

    Terminal
    $ OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 ollama serve

    On Windows, add OLLAMA_HOST as an environment variable and restart Ollama.

  3. 3

    Open Pacachat

    Allow local network access when iOS asks. Pacachat finds your computer, you pick a model, and you can start chatting.

The only cloud involved is this one.

When you send a message, it goes from your phone to your computer over your own network. It isn't sent to us or anyone else. Your chats are saved on your phone, and that's the only place they're saved.

  • No account to create, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads.
  • Nothing stored on our servers. We don't run any.
  • Delete a chat and it's gone. Delete the app and so is everything else.
  • The details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Stuck on setup, found a bug or have an idea? Email pacachat-support@whiskeydanger.com. You can also send feedback from Settings in the app.

What do I need before I start?

An iPhone or iPad, plus a computer running Ollama on the same network. Any Mac, Windows or Linux machine works. Bigger models need more memory, but small ones like llama3.2 run on most laptops.

Does it cost anything?

No. Pacachat is free, and so is Ollama. The models run on hardware you already own, so there's no per-message fee either.

Pacachat can't find my computer. What now?

Check three things. Your phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi. Ollama was started with OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0. And Pacachat has local network access, under Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network on your iPhone.

If it still doesn't show up, type the address in yourself, such as http://192.168.1.24:11434.

Does it work when I'm away from home?

Pacachat is built for your home network. If you already reach your computer remotely through a VPN, you can enter that address instead.

Which models can I use?

Any model you've downloaded in Ollama, such as Llama, Gemma, Mistral, Qwen or DeepSeek. Pacachat lists them for you, and you can switch from the top of any chat.

Where are my chats stored?

On your phone, inside the app. Your computer answers each message, but Pacachat keeps the history. Delete a chat in the app and it's gone.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Pacachat is on iPhone and iPad for now.

Why an alpaca?

Ollama's mascot is a llama. Alpacas are their smaller, fluffier cousins, and this one carries your messages.
